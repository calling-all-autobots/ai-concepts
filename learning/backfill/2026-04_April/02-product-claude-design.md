# Claude Design (research preview)

- **Category:** ① Product release
- **Date:** 2026-04-17  ·  **What it affects:** New Anthropic Labs product surface; Pro/Max/Team/Enterprise plans; powered by Claude Opus 4.7
- **Sources:** https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-design-anthropic-labs · https://techcrunch.com/2026/04/17/anthropic-launches-claude-design-a-new-product-for-creating-quick-visuals/

## In one line
Claude Design is a new Anthropic Labs product that turns prompts, images, documents, or even a codebase into interactive prototypes, slides, mockups, one-pagers, and landing pages — by talking to Claude.

## What actually changed
On Apr 17, 2026, one day after Opus 4.7 shipped, Anthropic released **Claude Design** in research preview on the Pro, Max, Team, and Enterprise plans (rolling out through the day). It is **powered by Claude Opus 4.7**, Anthropic's most capable vision model — the same model whose vision resolution jumped to 2,576px. You describe what you want; Claude produces a first version; you refine it with direct edits or further requests. It can **automatically apply your own design system**, and coverage noted a **Canva export** path. Anthropic positions it squarely at "founders and product managers without a design background" who need to communicate an idea visually.

## Why it matters
This is Anthropic moving from "model + API" toward finished end-user products in a specific vertical (the design/creative tooling market). For a PM, the interesting signal is twofold: (1) Opus 4.7's stronger vision is being productized directly, not just exposed as an API capability; and (2) Anthropic is willing to compete in application layers (design, alongside Cowork for knowledge work) rather than leaving them entirely to partners. Research-preview status means it is a directional bet, not a stable dependency.

## Your point of view
- "Claude Design is the consumer-facing proof of Opus 4.7's vision gains — the model upgrade and the product launched a day apart on purpose."
- "It targets the exact person I often am: someone who needs a credible mockup or one-pager fast without a designer."
- "It's a research preview from Anthropic Labs — great for speed, not something to build a production workflow around yet."

## What to do
- If you have Pro/Max/Team/Enterprise, try it for your next prototype, pitch one-pager, or slide draft and judge how close the first pass gets.
- Feed it your existing design system / brand assets to test the "auto-apply your design system" claim before trusting it for external-facing work.
- Treat outputs as drafts to refine, and keep a real design review in the loop for anything customer-facing.
