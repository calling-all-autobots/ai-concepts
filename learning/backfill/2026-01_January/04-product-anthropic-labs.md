# Anthropic Labs: a dedicated team to incubate experimental Claude products

- **Category:** ① Product release
- **Date:** 2026-01-11 (the candidate date; the official announcement page is dated 2026-01-13) · **What it affects:** Anthropic's product org / where new Claude surfaces come from
- **Sources:** https://www.anthropic.com/news/introducing-anthropic-labs

## In one line
Anthropic stood up "Labs," an org whose whole job is to tinker on experimental products at the frontier of Claude's capabilities, test them with early users, and scale the ones that work.

## What actually changed
In mid-January 2026 (the candidate list marks Jan 11; the official page carries a Jan 13 date) Anthropic introduced **Labs** — a dedicated team for building experimental products, operating on a "break the mold and explore" mandate distinct from the core research and platform roadmap. Its model is explicitly: tinker, test with early users, and responsibly scale what succeeds.

Leadership named: **Mike Krieger** (Instagram co-founder, Anthropic's Chief Product Officer) building alongside **Ben Mann** (Anthropic co-founder); **Ami Vora** leads the broader Product org with CTO **Rahul Patil**. Anthropic credited this incubation approach with past hits: **Claude Code** (grew to a ~billion-dollar product in six months), **MCP / Model Context Protocol** (~100M monthly downloads), **Skills**, **Claude in Chrome**, and **Cowork** — the last launched as a research preview the same week.

## Why it matters
This is an organizational, not a technical, release — but it's a strong forward signal. Anthropic is institutionalizing the "0-to-1 product" function that already produced Claude Code and MCP, which means a faster, more deliberate cadence of research previews aimed squarely at new user segments (Cowork being the immediate example). For anyone tracking where the platform is going, Labs is the pipeline to watch: the next few "research preview" surprises will disproportionately come from here.

## Your point of view
